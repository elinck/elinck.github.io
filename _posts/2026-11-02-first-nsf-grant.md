## The Linck Lab is NSF Funded!

Late news due to a busy semester, but the Linck Lab was [recently awarded an NSF grant](https://www.montana.edu/ecology/article.html?id=25478) to study speciation when diverging populations alternate between periods of geographic connection and isolation. This work will involve developing new theory and software and applying it genomic data from islands in Melanesia intermittently connected during periods of low sea level. Details on the the award [are available via NSF](https://www.nsf.gov/awardsearch/show-award/?AWD_ID=2617981). I'm currently hiring a postdoc to support work on the project; while the deadline for full consideration has passed, I continue to accept applications, so please reach out if you are interested.
