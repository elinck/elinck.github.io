summary: "We show that breeding Painted Buntings belong to three genetic clusters with distinct wintering ranges. Unexpectedly, contiguous populations in the interior southern United States are separated by a migratory divide, with birds from Louisiana wintering in the Yucatán, while birds from Texas and Oklahoma winter on the south coast of Mexico and in Central America. Birds from the southeastern United States remain allopatric throughout the annual cycle."
