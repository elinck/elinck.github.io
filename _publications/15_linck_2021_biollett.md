summary: "Closely related tropical bird species typically have similar elevational ranges and experience similar climatic conditions, while species that differ in these traits tend to be more distantly related. We argue this pattern suggests tropical vertebrates will track changing climate conditions instead of adapting in place, requiring protected habitat corridors to higher elevations and latitudes."
