summary: "We show that closely related terrestrial New Guinea kingfishers that live at different elevations likely diverged in isolation but have since hybridized, possibly counteracted by natural selection across mountainsides. We review studies of other bird species pairs with similar distributions and find limited evidence for gene flow during the initial stages of divergence, casting doubt on widespread paraptric speciation."
