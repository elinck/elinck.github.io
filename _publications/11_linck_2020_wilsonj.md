summary: "We describe variation in abundance, species richness, and functional diversity in karst forest bird communities in the Mariana Islands in Micronesia. We find the island of Rota has high functional diversity but fewer species and birds per hectare. Given the extinction of almost all forest birds on nearby Guam, we suggest continued monitoring is a conservation priority in the archipelago."
