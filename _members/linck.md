description: "Ethan is a broadly trained biologist interested in the origin and distribution of genetic variation and species. He also likes to think about environmental ethics and the societal role of biodiversity science and conservation. He grew up in Vermont (with a short but formative stint in Tucson) and received a B.A. in Biology from Reed College in Portland, Oregon, where he was introduced to the joys of genetics by [Sarah Schaack](https://www.reed.edu/biology/professors/schaack.html). After postbac research with [Jack Dumbacher](https://www.jackdumbacher.com/) and [Rob Guralnick](https://www.gurlab.net/) (and serving as winter caretaker at the Rocky Mountain Biology Laboratory), he did his Ph.D. in the Department of Biology at University of Washington in Seattle, working with [John Klicka](https://klickalab.wordpress.com/john-klicka/). Following grad school he was a postdoc at University of Tennessee, Knoxville (working remotely with [Kimberly Sheldon](https://www.biogeographyresearch.org/) and crashing [Scott Taylor's](https://www.colorado.edu/lab/taylor/) lab meetings in Boulder), University of New Mexico (working with [Chris Witt](https://www.unmornithology.org/)), and University of Wyoming (working with [Matt Carling](https://carlinglab.com/) and collaborating closely with [Zac Cheviron](https://www.chevironlab.org/)). In his free time he likes to [write](https://ethanlinck.substack.com/), [run](/running/), ski, and gently tease his toddler. [Click here](https://github.com/elinck/elinck_CV/blob/master/elinck_CV.pdf) for his CV."
